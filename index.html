Amazon Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Amazon Buddy does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal user data.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in local storage API. Your saved lists and products never leave your browser.

Data We Access

The extension reads product information (title, price, image, and URL) from Amazon pages only when you initiate a save action. This data is stored locally for your personal use.

What We Do NOT Do

  • No data is sent to any external server or third party
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used
  • We do not access, read, or store your browsing history
  • We do not collect personal information of any kind
  • We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any user data

Permissions Explained

  • Storage: Saves your lists and settings locally on your device
  • Active Tab: Reads product info from the Amazon page you are currently viewing
  • Scripting: Extracts product details when you click save
  • Tabs: Opens product pages and detects Amazon pages
  • Host Permissions (Amazon domains): Adds "Add to List" buttons on Amazon pages across all supported regions

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or external resources.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
